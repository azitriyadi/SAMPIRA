### Hero
Landscape hero composition, large standalone artwork at high resolution around 1536x1024 or larger. Compact forest-green collection van behind them, a leafy tree and small loose foliage framing scene, a second crate with a rolled cable and a small screen. No recycling text or branding. Large readable central subjects, excellent polished anatomical drawing. Isolated coherent scene on transparent background so page pattern can show around it.
